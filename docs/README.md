# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the index for OctoAcme's project management documentation.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a lightweight, iterative process to guide work from idea through delivery and continuous improvement:

- **Initiation:** Define the problem, goals, success criteria, stakeholders, and whether the work should proceed to planning.
- **Planning:** Break approved work into shippable increments, prioritize and estimate the backlog, and agree on responsibilities, milestones, dependencies, and the Definition of Done.
- **Execution & Tracking:** Deliver and review work in small increments, track progress on the project board, and surface blockers through the team's regular delivery rhythm.
- **Risk & Communication:** Maintain and monitor a risk register, share regular stakeholder updates, and escalate blockers or decisions through defined paths.
- **Release & Deployment:** Complete release checks, deploy and verify the change, communicate the release, and keep a rollback or mitigation plan ready.
- **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement:** Capture lessons after delivery milestones and turn them into owned, measurable actions that the team follows up on.
